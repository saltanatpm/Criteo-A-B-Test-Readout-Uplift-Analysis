# Criteo A/B Testing & Uplift Analysis

## Project Overview

An end-to-end experimentation and uplift modeling project using real-world Criteo advertising data.

The project demonstrates how to evaluate an A/B experiment, quantify incremental impact, estimate treatment effects, and translate statistical findings into actionable business recommendations.

## Business Objective

Determine whether a marketing intervention generates incremental conversions and whether its impact justifies deployment.

Key business questions:
- Does the treatment produce a statistically significant lift in conversion?
- What is the incremental conversion rate?
- How much additional business value does the treatment generate?
- Are there heterogeneous treatment effects across customer segments?
- Should the business launch, reject, or partially roll out the treatment?

## Project Structure

- `data/` — raw and processed datasets
- `notebooks/` — exploratory analysis, A/B testing, and uplift modeling
- `src/` — reusable analytical modules
- `reports/` — visualizations and business recommendations
- `tests/` — analytical validation and unit tests

## Methodology

1. Data exploration and quality validation
2. Experiment design and metric definition
3. A/B test statistical analysis
4. Confidence intervals and effect size estimation
5. Uplift modeling and heterogeneous treatment effects
6. Business impact estimation
7. Final go/no-go recommendation

## Final Deliverable

A business-oriented decision memo with a recommendation to launch, reject, or partially roll out the treatment, supported by statistical evidence and estimated incremental business impact.
